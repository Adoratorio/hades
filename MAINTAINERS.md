# Maintainers

Current maintainers of this library:

- Andrea Gottardi — [@AndreaGottardi](https://github.com/AndreaGottardi)
- Daniele Borra — [@borradaniele](https://github.com/borradaniele)
- Andrea Biason — [@biazo5](https://github.com/biazo5)

A change should be reviewed by a maintainer other than its author. Agree on a
release owner and a backup before each release. Repository access and npm
publishing rights are managed separately from this document and CODEOWNERS.

Historical contributors remain credited in package.json and the Git history,
including people who no longer work at Adoratorio. Maintenance responsibility
does not replace historical authorship. Corrections to credits are welcome;
verify identities before adding mailmap entries or merging aliases.

The mailmap normalizes names sharing an already documented work email. Other
email aliases remain separate until their identity is verified.
